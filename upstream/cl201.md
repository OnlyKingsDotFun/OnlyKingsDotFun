Stacked on #199 (token collections). Companion to the CP-Swap collection pools PR.

## what

A pair that contains a collection's quote mint can bind to that collection and become a **collection pool**: the other collection members get their own vaults under the pool, and any two members, the pair's own base token included, swap **directly against each other inside the pool**. A stable is a stable, an LST is an LST, a meme is a meme: members of one collection trade on the pool's own terms, not through the quote.

- `init_pool_members(amp)`: bind the pair (pool owner or admin). The pool token that is not the quote becomes member 0 and keeps its existing vault and fee fields.
- `add_pool_member`: permissionless; the mint must already be a member of the collection and match the base's decimals. Creates a vault owned by the pool state.
- `intra_swap(from, to, amount_in, min_out)`: any member to any member. Priced by a StableSwap over the members' rate-normalised reserves, so the price is exactly the collection rate (1:1 unless the collection set a rate) when the pool is balanced and bends against the trader as it skews. Fee is `trade_fee_rate / rebalance_fee_divisor`, floored at 1 ppm, taken on input and split protocol / fund like any swap. Base-side fees accrue on `PoolState`, member fees on `PoolMembers`.
- `collect_member_fees`: config owner / fund owner / admin collects member-vault fees.

The tick curve against the quote (extra swaps) is unchanged.

## why a curve and not the flat rate

At the flat rate a pool is a free option on every external price move: arbs drain the now-cheap member at the stale rate until only the worst asset is left. The StableSwap keeps the rate as the price at balance and makes extraction cost more the further the pool is pushed. The test buys out a member vault with 30x its size in base and gets less than the reserve for it; the vault never reaches zero.

## also in this branch: `Lst` rule kind

A fourth rule kind for stake-pool LSTs: the proof is the SPL stake pool account (owned by the ruleset's program, `account_type == StakePool`, `pool_mint == mint`). Members register at the pool's live exchange rate, `total_lamports / pool_token_supply`, and anyone can re-read it with `sync_member_rate` (no signer). An LST collection therefore never needs an authority-set rate and its intra swaps price LSTs against each other at their redemption values.
